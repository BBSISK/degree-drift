# TikTok / Reels / Shorts test: "1 in 3 Arts grads started in retail"

Video: `output/degree-drift-arts-tiktok.mp4` (1080×1920, 17 s, silent audio track so you can add a voiceover in the app).

All figures: CSO table HEO08, Arts & Humanities honours-degree (NFQ Level 8) graduates of 2010–2013, the same group followed from Year 1 to Year 10. Re-run `python3 video/scripts/build_tiktok_data.py` to check them against the live CSO table.

| Year after graduation | Retail | Education |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 32% | 11% |
| 3 | 18% | 25% |
| 5 | 11% | 32% |
| 10 | 7% | 36% |

## Voiceover script (record in the app's Voiceover tool)

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | "1 in 3 Arts grads started out in RETAIL" | "One in three Arts graduates started out working in retail." |
| 3–4.5 s | "Same graduates. 10 years later…" | "Same graduates. Ten years later…" |
| 4.5–9 s | Bars move: Year 1 → 3 → 5 → 10 | "Year three, teaching takes off. Year five, more again." |
| 9–13.5 s | "36% now work in education" | "By year ten, thirty-six percent work in education, and only seven percent are still in retail." |
| 13.5–17 s | "What did you study? Comment it 👇" | "What did you study? Comment below and I'll show you where it leads." |

Tip: say it slightly faster than feels natural. Optional opener if you'd rather show your face first: "I'm a maths teacher, and I looked at ten years of Irish graduate data."

## What to watch after posting

In TikTok Studio, compare the retention chart with the first video (where about half of viewers left in the first second). If more people are still watching at the 3-second mark, the hook-first format works, and it becomes the template for the subject-by-subject series.
