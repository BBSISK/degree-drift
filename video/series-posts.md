# Subject series: "Same graduates, 10 years later"

Five vertical videos (1080×1920, 17 s, silent audio track for an in-app voiceover) in `output/series/`.

All figures: CSO table HEO08, honours-degree (NFQ Level 8) graduates of 2010–2013, the same group followed from Year 1 to Year 10. Sector shares exclude "not stated". To re-check against the live CSO table: `python3 video/scripts/build_series_data.py`. Each video's hook also checks its own claim when rendering, so a CSO revision that makes a claim untrue stops the render.

Every caption ends the same way, so paste this credit under each one:

```
Data: CSO Ireland, HEO08 (honours grads of 2010–13, followed Year 1 to Year 10). © Govt of Ireland, CC BY 4.0. My own analysis. Full dashboard: link in bio.
```

Suggested order: ICT → Business → Science → Social Sciences → Engineering (strongest surprises first).
